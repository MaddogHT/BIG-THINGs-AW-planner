How the ratings work

Choose a tile, then its defender, to see the top 10 attackers with a support pick. Attackers are limited to champions currently available as 7★. A manual rank (📌) set on a pick overrides the rating order for that tile and defender.

Information used comes from:

  • Champion Spotlight Information from playcontestofchampions.com
  • Champion Information & Synergies from mcochub.insaneskull.com
  • Strong Counters and Strong Matchups from mcoc.gg
  • MaddogHT's Attack & Defense Champion Rankings
  • Season Guide Picks
How the list is ordered
  1. Champions MaddogHT has skipped are hidden.
  2. Champions he has ranked (📌) take the spots he gave them.
  3. Champions he has starred (★) come next.
  4. Everyone else is listed by rating, highest first. Champions at a class disadvantage come last, unless fewer than 10 other champions can do the fight.
How the rating is built
  • Tile fit: A champion earns points only if they can earn Force Charges on that tile. The amount varies by tile. On Tiles 1, 4, 6 and 7, any champion can earn charges, so everyone starts with a base rating of +3, +2, +3 and +3.
  • The bonuses below count only for champions who can already earn charges on that tile.
  • Class wheel: Cosmic beats Tech, Tech beats Mutant, Mutant beats Skill, Skill beats Science, Science beats Mystic, and Mystic beats Cosmic.

What "Can solo" means

MaddogHT or a user has indicated that the fight has been solo'd using that champion, either from actual in-game experience or common sense.

Other rules
  • Tile 1 (Dexterity): Every champion has equal access to dodging, so it is ranked by class advantage and the other bonuses. Champions that rely on Stun lose 1 point, because the defender is Stun-immune.
  • Tile 2 (Buff Safety): Iron Man and Red Skull are excluded, because their Nullify immunity comes from character abilities that do not count. Mangog gets a Nullify-immunity bonus only with Odin as his support.
  • Tile 3 (Bandaid Solution): Heal Block, Petrify, Specter and regeneration reversal or steal all count. Count Nefaria, The Leader and Void are reversal champions. Poison and Despair do not count.
  • Defender immunities (Tiles 3 and 8): If a defender is immune to every effect a champion would use, that champion's rating drops sharply (Heal Block, Petrify and Specter on Tile 3; Nullify, Stagger and Neutralize on Tile 8). Regeneration reversal is not affected by Heal Block immunity.
  • Tile-specific attack rankings: The Leader is ranked 4 on Tile 3 and 2 on every other tile.
  • Class disadvantage: Champions at a class disadvantage are listed last, and only when fewer than 10 other options can do the fight.
